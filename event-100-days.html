Customs & Traditions

We will feature a few small Chinese-inspired traditions during the celebration to bless Sai for his future:

🥚 Rolling the egg on Sai's feet (滚鸡蛋)

We will gently roll a red egg over Sai's little feet as a blessing for good fortune, happiness, and a smooth journey through life. In Chinese traditions, red eggs symbolize new life, while the rolling motion brings to mind 好事滚滚来 — may good things and blessings keep rolling his way.

🔴 The red dot (点朱砂)

Similar to an Indian tika, we will place a small dot of cinnabar on Sai's forehead as part of a traditional Chinese blessing called 点朱砂. This is a blessing for babies of all genders. The red mark symbolizes auspiciousness, wisdom, and protection, meant to wish Sai a bright and fortunate future.

🍜 Longevity noodles (长寿面)

We will begin our lunch with longevity noodles. Long noodles symbolize a wish for a lasting, vibrant life. This is our way of wishing Sai a long and joyful future surrounded by family and friends.
